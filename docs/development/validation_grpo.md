## Math,masking and verifier checks

Independent NumPy tests cover population group normalization,constant groups,
clipped-surrogate sign branches,per-completion weighting,padding and analytic
policy gradients. Old/reference logps and advantages receive no gradient. SymPy
checks exp(x)-x-1 value/gradient0 at0 and positive curvature exp(x);see
[evidence](evidence/grpo-symbolic.json). Pure loss references preserve FP64;
trainer rewards/behavior logps are explicitly FP32,independent of ambient dtype.

Sampling policy is byte-hashed before/after rollout collection;all outputs complete
before the update. Old logps are recorded for the exact sampled actions and agree
with naive full-prefix recomputation. Prompt/PAD tokens are excluded;every sampled
stop action is included. Group identity is window plus row-index//G under the
fixed recipe. Temperature1 and one update/fresh batch avoid a hidden behavior-
distribution mismatch;other sampling/stale-epoch modes are unimplemented.

The exact Fraction verifier has130-character/60-digit bounds,zero tolerance and
one-second post-call budget. It executes no generated code. Tests cover hostile
text,nonfinite rewards,overflow,malformed fractions,partial correct answers,
prematureEOS,empty/constant groups and mocked deadline expiry. A whole-window
all-zero-reward test checks zero Adam updates,empty optimizer state,unchanged
weights and correct resume. A post-call timer is not arbitrary-code preemption.

Near-zero scalar surrogate at fresh ratio1 is expected after group centering;
it can have nonzero policy gradients. The acceptance asserts gradients/actual
weight changes,not a decreasing CE-style RL loss. KL-only/zero-signal windows
are explicit counters and are not relabeled as successful policy learning.
